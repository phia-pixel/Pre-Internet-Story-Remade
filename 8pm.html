An image of my mother in the 1980s.

As she was returning to her apartment, she passed a stranger, a man only slightly older than she was, who spontaneously gave her a small bouquet of red and white carnations - Christmas flowers. She doesn’t remember exactly what he said, but it was something along the lines of “there is nothing wrong with giving flowers to strangers.” He then continued on his own path with no further expectations. The unexpected gift cheered her up a lot.
